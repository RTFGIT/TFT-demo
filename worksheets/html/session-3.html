Session 3 · Eat More Fruit & Veg · Classroom task

Higher / Lower Game: water in food production

  1. Show and name the food item on the large card – the litres of water used to produce it are revealed by lifting the flap.
  2. Read the amount, then show the next card: will this food item be higher or lower?
  3. Encourage the team to shout “higher” or “lower”.
  4. Reveal to check the answer – were they correct? Were they surprised?
