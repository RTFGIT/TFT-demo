Session 4 · Eat Food That Keeps You Fuller and More Energised For Longer · Classroom task

Energise The Team or Sugar Lump Top Trumps

Energise The Team

  1. It’s time to energise the team – sort the foods into simple and complex carbohydrates.
  2. Discuss choices and let them make any corrections.
  3. Which foods would you swap to feel fuller and more energised for longer?

Sugar Lump Top Trumps

  1. Play in two teams or in pairs. Compare the sugar content of each food item – the card with the most sugar wins.
  2. Which foods are high in sugar? Were there any surprises?
