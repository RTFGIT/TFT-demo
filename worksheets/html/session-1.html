Session 1 · Greenhouse Gases / Climate Change · Classroom task

Match Time

  1. Use a timer to keep the team on task.
  2. Discuss the answers and let them make any corrections.

In pairs or small groups – read the cards and match the words to the definitions.

  1. AAtmosphere5
  2. BClimate1
  3. CClimate Change6
  4. DGreenhouse Gases3
  5. EMethane4
  6. FCarbon Dioxide2
  1. 1The average weather and temperature of a place over a long period of time, usually 30 years.
  2. 2The greenhouse gas produced by burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and fuel in cars, factories and power stations.
  3. 3The gases in the Earth’s atmosphere, such as carbon dioxide (CO₂), methane and nitrous oxide. They trap heat from the sun and keep the Earth warm, similar to how a greenhouse works.
  4. 4The greenhouse gas produced by cows and other animals when they burp or release gas.
  5. 5The envelope of gases that surrounds the Earth and makes life possible on the planet.
  6. 6The long-term shift in temperatures and weather patterns in a region or globally. In the last 200 years human activity has increased greenhouse gas emissions, trapping heat, warming the Earth and causing climate change.
